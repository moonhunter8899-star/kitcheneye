KitchenEye

Phone line-judge for pickleball. Scan the court, tap the ball, get IN / OUT calls.

Multi-phone: one phone hosts, up to three join by code, all judge together.

Uses the rear camera. Video never leaves your phone. Needs HTTPS (or localhost) for camera access.

Multi-phone match

One phone hosts and shows a code; the others join it. Every phone scans the court from its own spot, then all judge together — the closest phone's call wins.
